# RoleLogic v1.20.1

**Release Date:** November 7, 2026

---

## What's New

### Role Link Slots Go to Members Who Are in the Server

On the free plan, a Role Link gives its role to the first 100 users on the integration's list. Someone who had left the server or been banned kept their place among those 100 for as long as the integration kept listing them, so the next person in line never got the role. Removing them did not help either: the integration put them back on its next sync.

Now **users who are not in the server don't take a slot**:

- **The next person in line gets the role.** On each sync (about every 30 minutes on free) the bot checks who on the list is in the server. A slot held by someone who left, was kicked or banned, or never joined goes to the next user in line in that same sync.
- **Nothing to remove by hand.** Keep the list exactly as your integration sends it. If someone comes back, they get their slot back when they rejoin.
- **Shown on the card.** The role link card says how many people on the list aren't in the server, and the "waiting to sync" count no longer includes them.
- **No extra load.** Only free links over their 100-user limit do the check, inside the sync the bot already runs. Premium links, and links at or under the limit, work exactly as before.
- **For integration developers.** [`GET /users`](../reference/role-link-api#list-users) now reports `absent_count`, and `synced_count` and `pending_count` leave those users out. The reference for that endpoint now shows the response it actually returns.

Also fixed: when an integration added a user beyond the 100th slot, that user briefly got the role until the next sync took it away. Now they wait for a slot like everyone else.

### Video Guides

- **A guide on every dashboard page.** Each page has a **Watch guide** button for the video that covers it, and the player lists the whole series, so any guide is one click away. Paused and scheduled rules link to the guide on going live. Narration is in English; other languages say so under the video and keep English captions.
- **Integration guides.** The osu! Player Role, Twitch Follower Role, Form Respondent Role, Kick Channel Role and Birthday Role pages now show a step-by-step video.
- **A fuller homepage tour.** The homepage explainer now walks through AND rules, booster glow, integrations, cross-server roles and more ideas.

---

### Pay with PayPal

Card checkout cannot take PayPal for RoleLogic's location, so checkout now offers **Prefer PayPal?** It takes you to the Patreon tier that grants the same slots, linked to your Discord account. Patreon prices now include the same 8% SST card checkout adds, so both ways cost the same. Checkout tells you when something differs for you: monthly billing on an annual plan, a promo code that won't carry over, or a Patreon tier that would be replaced rather than added.

---

### No More Cookie Banner

The cookie banner is gone. From November 2, 2026 the website measures visits with Google Analytics for everyone. The statistics are not linked to your Discord account, and you can turn them off with the switch on the [Privacy page](https://rolelogic.faizo.net/privacy#cookies). The privacy policy, in all seven languages, explains this.

## Improvements and Fixes

- **Reaction-role bots no longer stop your rules.** When another bot rewrote a member's whole role list from an old copy, undoing several of RoleLogic's roles at once, each role counted as a separate revert and could trip the safety stop for the whole server. That kind of update now counts as one.
- **Integrations that add the same member twice at once** no longer get an error back.
- **Your server list loads more reliably.** The dashboard retries Discord after a timeout or a Discord-side error, and opening a server you don't manage now says so instead of failing with an error.
- **Staying signed in.** Two tabs renewing your session at the same moment no longer sign you out everywhere.
- **Unsaved-changes prompt.** The "leave without saving?" question no longer crashes the page in Firefox.
- **Steadier background syncs.** The bot retries a member page after a dropped connection or a Discord server error instead of skipping the server until the next cycle, and role link logs stop at the first deleted or inaccessible log channel instead of trying it once per member.
- **Fully translated.** Everything new in this release is available in English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Malay, and Brazilian Portuguese.
